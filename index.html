NOTRE HÉRITAGE

Two Centuries of
Uncompromising Art.

Founded in 1842 along Rue de la Paix, Maison AURORE was commissioned by European sovereigns to fashion bespoke crystal presentation cases and celestial chronometers.

Today, our master craftsmen and nose continue this unbroken tradition. Every formula is aged for nine months in French oak casks before being individually numbered and sealed by hand.
