Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 4, 2026
[CLIENT/LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]: This Privacy Policy outlines baseline transparent data processing standards for LUXÉ. Prior to final production deployment, client legal counsel should review and verify jurisdictional specificities (e.g. GDPR, CCPA, CalOPPA).

1. Introduction

Welcome to LUXÉ ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our guests, clients, and website visitors. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and your rights.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly through our inquiry, booking, or subscription forms:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Full name, email address, telephone number, and postal address.
  • Service & Reservation Preferences: Table bookings, suite preferences, requested appointment times, event inquiries, or project briefs.
  • Communication Records: Correspondence, feedback, and customer support inquiries.

3. Technical & Usage Information

When navigating our digital platforms, we automatically collect basic server log data necessary for site performance and security:

  • Standard HTTP server headers, IP address, browser type, and operating system.
  • Referral pages, timestamp of visit, and pages requested.
  • Essential technical cookies required for session stability, cart preservation, or reservation state management.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate commercial and customer-fulfillment purposes:

  • Fulfilling customer reservations, client orders, or service discovery inquiries.
  • Transmitting important reservation confirmations or updates.
  • Safeguarding our digital infrastructure against fraudulent requests, spam bots, and security vulnerabilities.
  • Complying with applicable statutory accounting and tax regulations.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We implement a privacy-first cookie policy. Non-essential tracking cookies are not deployed without affirmative user consent. You can modify or withdraw your cookie preferences at any time using our Cookie Consent Banner or through your web browser configuration.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is shared strictly with vetted operational infrastructure service providers (such as hosting servers, transactional email relays, or payment gateways) under strict data protection agreements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you maintain specific statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access and review information retained about you.
  • Right to request rectification of inaccurate records.
  • Right to request erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") of your personal data.
  • Right to restrict or object to certain processing activities.
